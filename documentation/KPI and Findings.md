# KPI Definitions

### Total Swaps
Number of successful battery swap transactions.

### Total Revenue
Total revenue generated from successful battery swaps.

### Average Wait Time
Average number of minutes customers wait before receiving service.

### Average Swap Duration
Average time required to complete a battery swap.

### Battery Shortage Events
Number of service events where batteries available before the swap were 2 or fewer.

### Total Downtime
Total number of station downtime minutes.

### Service Success Rate
Percentage of service events that resulted in a successful battery swap.

### Failed Service Events
Number of service events where the battery swap was unsuccessful.



# Findings

- Total network swaps: **30,000**
- Total revenue: approximately **GHS 1.07M**
- Average wait time: approximately **3.9 minutes**
- Battery shortage rate: approximately **5.9%**
- Total downtime: approximately **116.2 hours**
- Adenta recorded the highest transaction volume.
- Adenta also recorded the highest downtime.
- Demand shows a strong evening peak, especially around the early evening period.
